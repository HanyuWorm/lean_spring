# LLD Interview Canvas

## 1. Clarify (5–8 phút)

- Actors, top use cases, scale/concurrency vừa đủ.
- Scope/non-goals, assumptions.

## 2. Invariants (5 phút)

- Critical rules, authorization, duplicate/concurrent behavior.

## 3. Contracts và model (15 phút)

- Commands/queries/results/errors.
- Entity/value object/aggregate, responsibilities/collaborators.

## 4. Critical flow (10 phút)

- State table + sequence, transaction/concurrency.

## 5. Patterns/failures (10 phút)

- Variation boundary và pattern có lý do.
- Timeout/retry/idempotency/error.

## 6. Verify/trade-off (5 phút)

- Critical tests, alternatives, extension và deliberate omissions.
